# Accessibility research with real people — plan and funding gate

Prepared 21 October 2026 (America/Fortaleza). Item 202 preparation while 201 CI runs; execute and integrate after 201's autonomous checks. No participant contacted, recruited or tested; no account, quote request, contract or payment made. This plan is not accessibility certification. Existing synthetic matrices do not replace lived-experience research.

## Participants and rounds

Proposed minimum 18 distinct adults per round: three primarily using screen readers, three with low vision/magnification, three deaf or hard of hearing, three using alternative input because of motor access needs, three with cognitive/neurodivergent access needs and three older adults with varied digital confidence. These are design choices, not a W3C-mandated sample size or statistical representation. Needs overlap; do not double-count a person to claim 18. Record participants' own preferred access methods, not diagnoses. Include intersectional needs, novice/experienced users and the existing supported buyer languages when practically recruited. No new product translation is activated.

Round 1: 18 moderated individual sessions,60 minutes maximum with breaks or two 30-minute sessions at the participant's choice. Offer an accessible written/asynchronous equivalent; allow extra accommodation time without penalizing success metrics. Round 2 after fixes: repeat all 18 in the worst-case budget, prioritizing prior blockers and including new participants to detect learning effects. Two additional pilot sessions are budgeted separately, not silently counted as coverage. Small samples reveal barriers; they do not establish population-wide success rates.

Screen-reader coverage should include participants' familiar NVDA/JAWS on Windows and VoiceOver on Apple devices. Magnification includes actual browser 400% zoom and OS settings. Motor sessions include keyboard-only, voice/switch use as appropriate. Deaf sessions check caption/transcript availability and information without audio. Cognitive sessions check instructions, errors, interruption/recovery and pressure-free choices. Older participants are not assumed disabled. Device/software availability and specialist recruitment must be confirmed before contracting, not inferred from a supplier's general marketing.

## Tasks and safeguards

Use an isolated disposable study environment with synthetic creator, product, buyer, email inbox, payment provider and downloadable harmless file. Never ask a participant to connect their own Stripe, send an actual message, charge a card or publish a real business. Do not collect real customer records.

Each person chooses the creator or buyer task order; offer both in a second block if fatigue would otherwise distort results:
1. Find prices and explain the chosen plan's actual price, limits and sales availability.
2. Request sign-in using a study inbox, explicitly confirm and reach the empty Studio.
3. Create a synthetic product, correct a deliberate validation error and reload to verify persistence.
4. Preview and explicitly publish only the disposable study store; locate the resulting product and reverse the change.
5. Find a product, explain price/selected extras and complete simulated checkout.
6. Recover a purchase through the captured study inbox, open the buyer library and download/open the harmless file.
7. Find help, understand an error or resting state and recover without losing work.
8. When OP5 is eventually implemented and approved for research, add owned-source import → draft review → edit/undo → explicit publish, measuring clicks and elapsed time; do not claim this future flow works today.

Use neutral prompts such as “Show me how you would find what this costs.” Explain that the service is being tested, not the person. Do not coach a route until the unassisted attempt is recorded. Stop immediately on distress, privacy exposure or an accidental real endpoint. An inaccessible recording tool must never exclude a participant; permit a note-only session.

Record task completion (unassisted/assisted/not completed), blocking step, actual focus/reading order, input method, error recovery, misinterpreted price/consent, elapsed time excluding requested breaks, clicks/key actions and the participant's own description of effort. Record abandonment honestly. Do not rank disability groups by speed or average away blockers. Findings include exact commit, environment, viewport/device/AT versions, reproducible steps, expected outcome, severity and consented anonymized evidence.

A blocker affecting access, payment comprehension, privacy or preservation of work blocks release of that flow until fixed and retested. Lower-severity findings receive an owner and verification date. A successful retest documents that participant/task/version; it does not certify all people, devices or WCAG criteria. Combine human research with expert standards review, as [W3C WAI explains](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/involving-users/) (consulted 21 October 2026).

## Consent and data handling

Before scheduling, provide the accessible study purpose, tasks, duration, accommodations, incentive, withdrawal route, who observes and who accesses notes. Separate participation consent from optional audio/video/screen recording and quotation/publicity permission. Refusal of recording does not forfeit agreed compensation; no marketing opt-in is implied. Do not request medical evidence or disclose membership across stores.

Use participant codes in findings; keep scheduling/payment information separate with restricted access. No production identity or study recording enters model prompts or public issues. Proposed retention: raw recordings deleted 30 days after the final report, contact/payment information only for an agreed operational/legal retention schedule, anonymized issue records retained for regression tracking. These are proposed controls requiring owner/privacy review and supplier confirmation, not a declaration of an existing contractual or legal retention duty. Explain withdrawal limits before consent, provide accessible deletion/export requests and verify deletion in supplier copies. No data transfer agreement or terms accepted by this work.

## Total cost per round and funding calculation

For a quote covering 18 people, define in one currency:
R = recruitment/platform fee
S = sum of participant/session compensation
M = moderation, preparation, analysis and reporting labor
A = interpreters, captioning, accommodation and accessible materials
D = study devices, cloud, storage and secure transcription
P = pilots
V = taxes, cross-border charges, FX spread and payment fees under the more expensive Brazil/US reading
B = priced contingency reserve
Then C_round = R+S+M+A+D+P+V+B. Two-round worst case C_programme = C_round1+C_round2; do not assume free retest or omit internal labor. Monthly fixed funding must cover the actual cash schedule; annual funding includes all rounds/commitments. A12-month amortization may describe planning but does not pay an upfront invoice.

Known numeric lower bounds only: one Fable project starts atUSD 3,750; two such project floors totalUSD 7,500 if two are required, but neither establishes participant coverage. AccessWorks compensation floor isUSD 1,350/18-person round orUSD 2,700/two rounds before every other cost; two pilot tests add at leastUSD 150 if that compensation rule applies. Total round/programme costs remain UNKNOWN, not zero. The owner must receive a complete comparable quote and funded reserve before choosing.

For each plan/interval i, let r_i be revenue recognized for the relevant funded month, v_i all worst-month variable costs and taxes at 100% rights, and s_i=0.40*r_i-v_i. Require isolated-client net >= 60% before allocating research. Let F be existing fixed costs and C the additional research cash cost in that month. The exact consolidated gate is sum(n_i*s_i)>=F+C, with actual plan mix and all fiscal assumptions. For a single-plan case with s_i>0, N_i=ceil((F+C)/s_i). If s_i<=0, or any input is unknown, no finite approved customer minimum can be asserted. Annual plans use recognized monthly revenue and the stricter annual cash/reserve model; never treat the entire annual receipt as spare monthly margin.

The existing most-tax model remains below 60% and blocks sales; this research does not repair or certify it. Therefore the presently approved “minimum paying customers to fund this round” is NOT DETERMINABLE/NOT APPROVED. No honest fixed number can be produced from unquoted research cost, unknown taxes and failed isolated margin. Conditional arithmetic examples, not actual customer economics: at USD 10 of verified spare contribution per customer-month aUSD 3,750 floor needs at least375 customer-months before existing fixed costs; at USD 25 it needs150; at USD 50 it needs75. Full quoted C and F increase these floors. No price, visible limit, commission, tax gate, art. 9a review or PLANS_ON_SALE setting changes.
