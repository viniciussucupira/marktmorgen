# Conditional Spanish VAT and OSS evidence

Evidence snapshot: 2026-11-05. This note records official Spanish material for a possible choice of Spain as Member State of identification under the non-Union One Stop Shop and for supplies whose Member State of consumption is Spain. It does not determine the LLC's establishment, fixed-establishment position, customer status, place of supply, supplier or deemed-supplier status, registration, invoice facts or eligibility for a special scheme.

The existing Union-wide study remains `docs/tx4-oss-evidence.md`. This note narrows the Spanish evidence. It must not be read as a second tax layer over a transaction already accounted for under the legally applicable route.

## Decision boundary

The One Stop Shop is optional. The [Spanish Tax Agency's OSS overview](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-comercio-electronico/regimenes-especiales-ventanilla-unica.html), checked 2026-11-05, describes three optional regimes and limits them to supplies to final consumers. For the non-Union regime, a taxable person not established in the Union may choose Spain or another Member State as Member State of identification.

The current [Spanish VAT Act, Law 37/1992](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740), checked 2026-11-05, defines the non-Union scheme in Article 163 octiesdecies. It covers a person whose business establishment is outside the Union, who has no fixed establishment there, and who supplies services to persons who are not taxable persons where those services are located in the Union.

The [Tax Agency's 2025 VAT manual: non-Union scheme](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2025/capitulo-06-regimenes-especiales-iva/reg-espec-ventas-distancia-entreg-servic/regimen-exterior-union.html), checked 2026-11-05, repeats those definitions and distinguishes the Member State of identification from the Member State of consumption.

Those are conditions, not findings about Marktmorgen. This evidence does not determine that the LLC is not established in the Union, lacks a fixed establishment there, supplies a final consumer, makes a supply located in Spain or should choose Spain as Member State of identification. It does not select a place of supply, registration or service classification. Ordinary Spanish VAT registration and the non-Union OSS route are not interchangeable defaults.

## Model 369 filing qualifications

The Tax Agency's [Model 369 general guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-comercio-electronico/presentacion-autoliquidaciones-periodicas-modelo-369.html), checked 2026-11-05, records these qualifications:

- A person registered in Spain for the non-Union scheme submits Model 369 for each calendar quarter.
- The filing period is the following calendar month after the reporting period.
- A return remains required while registered even when no covered supply occurred. This is a zero return, not evidence of zero preparation, review, submission or payment cost.
- Once the scheme is chosen, Model 369 includes all covered supplies to which that regime applies; it is not a transaction-by-transaction election.
- Model 369 reports output VAT for covered supplies. It does not deduct input VAT; the guidance describes separate refund procedures, and other transactions may instead require local registration and ordinary returns.

The Tax Agency's [2026 calendar warning for non-working days](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/calendario-contribuyente/calendario-contribuyente-2026/recuerde/vencimientos-dias-inhabiles-sabados-festivos.html), checked 2026-11-05, expressly says the Model 369 deadline does not extend when its final day is a weekend or public holiday. That statement is calendar evidence for the scheme, not a calendar assigned to the LLC.

Article 163 noniesdecies states that, when Spain is the chosen Member State of identification, the quarterly return is electronic and the tax corresponding to it is paid within the return-filing period. This document does not calculate tax, choose a payment method, create a deadline, or authorize filing or payment.

## Invoice and operation-record boundaries

Article 163 noniesdecies of Law 37/1992 states that a person using the non-Union scheme must issue and deliver invoices adjusted to the regulatory requirements when Spain is the Member State of identification. That conditional Spanish rule is not a finding that the LLC uses the scheme or that a particular transaction requires a particular invoice. Invoice content, timing, language, currency and delivery remain unresolved.

The current [Spanish VAT Regulation, Royal Decree 1624/1992](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28925), checked 2026-11-05, lists the special-scheme operation-record content in Article 61 sexiesdecies. It includes the Member State of consumption, supply type and date, taxable amount and currency, later adjustments, VAT rate and amount, payments and advances, invoice information when issued, customer-location evidence and evidence of returns.

The Tax Agency's [standard electronic operation-record form](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/iva-comercio-electronico/nuevos-regimenes-especiales-ventanilla-unica/formulario-normalizado-presentacion-registros-operaciones.html), checked 2026-11-05, confirms that persons under the non-Union and Union regimes keep the specified records and provide them electronically when requested.

Law 37/1992 requires those records to be retained for ten years from the end of the year in which the transaction occurred and kept available to the Member State of identification and consumption. This is a special-scheme evidence minimum. It is not a universal ten-year deletion rule, does not shorten any longer duty or legal hold, and does not change retention, storage location or deletion code.
