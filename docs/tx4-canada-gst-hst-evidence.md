This note does not presume that designation was requested or granted, and does not select a payment method.

A due date on a Saturday, Sunday or CRA-recognized holiday moves to the next business day. These are reporting rules, not a calculated deadline for this LLC.

Different periods exist for particular records and situations; longer retention can apply, including unfiled returns, unresolved objections or appeals, or a CRA direction. Record-location requirements and any regime-specific exceptions need separate review. This is not a universal deletion date or permission to retain unrelated personal data.
