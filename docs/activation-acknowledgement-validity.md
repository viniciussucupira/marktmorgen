# Activation acknowledgement validity

AB(a), 2026-10-24. Checkout and switch activation must revalidate the current funding configuration and commercial review after awaiting their atomic Redis acknowledgement. The binding must still match and the clock must not precede the issuing instant. Existing acknowledgement validation still requires the exact current period and permitted activation deadline. This covers initial and idempotent committed observations; it cannot create a renewal.

A refused acknowledgement does not undo the Redis transaction: every receipt, active pointer, reserved cent and consumed slot remains committed. There is no release, refund, replenishment, new field/key, additional Redis call or provider request. This is additional local computation only, with unmeasured CPU cost: unknown cannot be zero or a certified margin. Monthly and annual plan assessments must retain full worst-case rights, highest-paid costs, spend floor, caps and reserves, the most onerous tax interpretation and all LLC fixed costs including preserved Hazelsong residuals. Commercial policy stays null; coverage flags stay false; PLANS_ON_SALE stays false.

Eight synthetic asynchronous cases cover checkout and switch crossed with admission expiry, month rollover, policy evidence mutation and a backwards clock. They assert refusal after a real stub commit, an identical complete committed ledger and only the original request. Existing positive, idempotent and lost-acknowledgement cases remain. Full remote CI on the exact new head is required before incorporation. No real financial or provider operation is permitted by these tests.
