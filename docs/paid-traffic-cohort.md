# Paid traffic resource cohort

AB(a), 24 October 2026. A period explicitly marked funded:true must acknowledge its paid resource window in both isResting and takePublicRequest after a valid allowance window and before folder/traffic lookup and request counting. Each entry path invokes the helper once. Legacy/trial behavior and displayed/billing counters are unchanged. Cleanup, billing and already-paid settlement are not gated here.

This gate refuses new marked-funded traffic work when actual approval is absent. It does not prove a supplier-byte/request bound or financial approval for all resources.

## Incremental cost before operation

One shared-cohort EVAL per marked funded traffic admission; at most 18 commands first/17 identical retry; serialized request JSON <=12,288 bytes. Same permanent cohort field as uploads/mail/AI/orders, no new media-specific field. One field/month or twelve/annual grant; no expiry/release of financial principal.

This scenario is not a proven global maximum. Preview/owner paths, repeated attempts, rejected requests and abuse must be bounded independently. Watched hours cannot bound player issuances, buffering, replay, concurrent tokens, downloaded bytes or supplier billing. Exact topology, CPU/memory, transfer/replies/HTTP/TLS overhead, retention, global cap and reserve remain unknown and fail approval. No cost is removed from the worst case. Most onerous tax reading and 100% LLC fixed-cost allocation remain binding. No 60% margin certification or actual policy approval; three coverage flags remain false and sales closed.
