# Conditional Italian VAT and OSS evidence

Evidence snapshot: 2026-11-05. This note records official source material for a possible Italian Member State of identification and for Italian consumption-state obligations. It does not determine the LLC's registration, place of supply, customer status, fixed-establishment position, deemed-supplier status or eligibility for a special scheme. It does not select any place of supply or service classification.

The existing cross-border study remains `docs/tx4-oss-evidence.md`. This note narrows the Italian evidence and must not be read as a second tax layer over a transaction already accounted for under the legally applicable route.

## Decision boundary

The non-Union scheme is optional. Under the current Article 74-quinquies text reproduced in Legislative Decree 83/2021, a taxable person domiciled or resident outside the European Union and not established in any Member State may identify in Italy for VAT obligations on services supplied in the European Union to non-taxable persons. The European VAT Directive separately defines the Member State of identification as the Member State chosen by the non-EU taxable person.

Those descriptions are conditions, not findings about Marktmorgen. The evidence does not determine that the LLC is outside the EU for this purpose, has no fixed establishment, supplies a non-taxable person, makes a supply located in Italy, or should choose Italy as Member State of identification. Ordinary Italian VAT registration and the non-Union OSS route are not interchangeable defaults.

## Official Italian sources checked

### Italian legal scope and portal

- [Legislative Decree 83/2021, official Normattiva text](https://www.normattiva.it/atto/caricaDettaglioAtto?atto.codiceRedazionale=21G00094&atto.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=2021-06-15&tipoDettaglio=originario), checked 2026-11-05, records the current Article 74-quinquies scope. It changes the heading to the special scheme for services supplied by non-EU persons, covers services supplied in the EU to non-taxable persons, and states that an eligible person may identify in Italy. It also records that Title II does not apply to a person using this scheme; if an invoice is issued, Articles 21 and following apply. That is a conditional invoice rule, not a direction to issue or suppress an invoice for the LLC.
- [Agenzia delle Entrate notice of 1 April 2021](https://telematici.agenziaentrate.gov.it/Main/Avviso?id=20210401214329), checked 2026-11-05, announces OSS/IOSS preregistration and the 1 July 2021 transition. It distinguishes the authenticated service from the non-EU VAT One Stop Shop area and lists consumer services as part of the post-transition scope.
- [Agenzia delle Entrate OSS calendar entry dated 30 April 2025](https://www1.agenziaentrate.gov.it/servizi/scadenzario/main.php/main.php?chi=3814&come=516&cosa=3098&entroil=30-04-2025&op=4), checked 2026-11-05, describes a return for the previous quarter, filed through the Portale OSS. Its taxpayer description uses the older telecommunications, broadcasting and electronic-services wording; the current statutory scope above controls any classification. The calendar entry says the filing remains due when the quarter contains no operations. In that case the scheme requires a nil return, not an invented taxable amount.
- [Current EUR-Lex consolidation of VAT Directive 2006/112/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2006/112/2025-04-14/eng), checked 2026-11-05, is the current consolidated version dated 14 April 2025. Articles 358a to 369 were rechecked against it: they define the chosen Member State of identification, cover services to non-taxable persons in the EU, require a quarterly return whether or not covered supplies occurred, and set the payment and record duties. The consolidation is documentary; the Directive and its amending acts are the legal framework.

The calendar entry is operational evidence for the named 2025 deadline. It is not a calendar assigned to the LLC and does not establish that an Italian filing is due.

## Filing and payment qualifications

The Italian calendar and the Commission's current OSS guidance must be read together:

- [Commission: Declare and pay in OSS](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop/declare-and-pay-oss_en), checked 2026-11-05, states that the non-Union and Union tax period is the calendar quarter and that the return is due by the end of the following month. It lists Q1 to 30 April, Q2 to 31 July, Q3 to 31 October and Q4 to 31 January, without extending the deadline for a weekend or public holiday.
- A return is required for each period even with no covered EU supplies; the Commission describes that as a nil return.
- The Agenzia entry says the Italian Portale OSS is used. It records payment by F24 without offset or by bank transfer under the portal instructions. The Commission says payment should accompany the return and has the same deadline as the return. The payment must carry the unique return reference so that it can be matched.
- The return is generally prepared in euro. If supplies use another currency, the Commission describes the European Central Bank exchange rate on the final date of the tax period. This evidence does not choose a currency conversion implementation for Marktmorgen.
- Corrections to periods beginning on or after 1 July 2021 are generally reported in a later OSS return. This does not authorize Marktmorgen to alter an already filed return or calculate a correction without a confirmed applicable route.

No filing date above becomes an LLC deadline until the owner-dependent facts and the legally correct route are established.

## Invoice and record boundaries

Article 74-quinquies as reproduced by Normattiva says Title II does not apply to a person using that special scheme and, if an invoice is issued, Articles 21 and following apply. This preserves both parts: it neither invents a general invoice requirement nor treats an invoice as outside Italian invoice rules.

[Commission: Record keeping and audits in OSS](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop/record-keeping-and-audits-oss_en), checked 2026-11-05, summarizes the detailed record content in Article 63c of Implementing Regulation 282/2011. The records include the Member State of consumption, type and date of supply, VAT payable, payments on account and the evidence used to locate the customer. They must be retained for ten years from the end of the year in which the transaction occurred and made available electronically without delay when requested by the Member State of identification or consumption.

The ten-year clock is an evidence-retention minimum for covered OSS transactions. It is not a universal ten-year deletion rule and does not shorten any longer duty, litigation hold or other applicable record period. This document does not change retention, storage location or deletion code.
