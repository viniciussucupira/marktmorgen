# What paid A/B results measure

The settled Stripe Checkout amount_total is the buyer's gross payment, including
any tax collected at checkout. The recorded sale amounts and squares do not
subtract refunds, disputes or payment fees. They are not net revenue or profit.

The editor now names gross payments in setup, per-version totals, probability and
declared-measure labels. A visible payment-basis explanation accompanies every
available paid result, including a winning result. The fourteen-day waiting period
is explicitly a waiting period, not a refund deduction.

No calculation, statistical boundary, sample floor, automatic allocation, refund
accounting, pricing, quota or fiscal/commercial gate changes. No claim of causal
sales improvement or certified margin. Unknown results keep their existing
unavailable notice, without fabricated payments.

Real-browser fixtures explicitly select a paid product and check the disclosure
with a maturing reading and a winner at 390/1280, including scoped accessibility
and fitting checks. Existing malformed-data, recovery and Path scenarios remain.
All provider interactions use synthetic data; no real payment or message.
