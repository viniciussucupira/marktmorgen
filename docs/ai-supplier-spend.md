# Anthropic supplier spend authorization

## Runtime behavior

Every askModel call now reserves a declared upper-bound dollar cost through ai-supplier-spend before fetch. isAiConfigured also requires a valid supplier policy, so absent or invalid policy disables configured writing availability. Direct askModel calls are guarded independently of that availability check. This is a deliberate fail-closed change: do not merge/deploy without genuine reviewed bounds and rollout validation. No production policy has been created.

AI_SUPPLIER_SPEND_POLICY is server-only JSON, at most 32,000 characters. Required fields: period (current UTC YYYY-MM), currency USD, unit microUSD (one million units = one US dollar), budgetMicroUsd (nonnegative safe integer), validUntil (future integer Unix milliseconds), evidence (nonempty reference), boundsConfirmed true, models (one to ten unique exact model IDs). Each model requires maxOutputTokens, maxRequestBytes and upperBoundMicroUsd as positive safe integers, plus evidence. The upper bound must cover the entire request at its allowed payload/output maximum, all applicable token/media/context pricing, fees, taxes charged by the supplier and any other supplier charge. Model configuration is an explicit allowlist. This code validates the declaration, not the truth/completeness of its evidence. Do not substitute historical average cost or visible answer length for a worst-case bound.

The cap is per Redis database and provider, shared by all models/stores/entry points through askModel. All deployments sharing a supplier account must use the same ledger or a rigorously partitioned sum of caps; independent databases do not provide one combined provider ceiling. The key is nl:spend:anthropic:<period>. It deliberately excludes model/policy revision/store and has no expiry or automatic decrement, so changing models or policy metadata cannot reset prior reservations. Retain and back up these keys. Redis data loss/deletion, restoration to stale snapshots, direct calls outside this helper or alternate supplier accounts invalidate continuity.

Authorization uses one INCRBY and accepts only one exact safe numeric counter at least as large as the reserved amount, within the budget. Unknown, lost, oversized or malformed replies authorize no fetch and are not retried. Failed provider calls, refusals and parsing errors retain their full reservation. Rejected over-budget increments also remain held; this is conservative and can exhaust availability early. Reservations are not actual expense recognition or proof of provider receipt. No automatic reconciliation or refund is implemented. The policy is rechecked for expiry/month rollover or changed configuration after the Redis reply.

The serialized request body is measured in UTF-8 bytes and reused for fetch. This includes system, messages, model and max_tokens; the configured bound must account for all content types accepted in that body. Unknown model IDs, token limits or request sizes outside the reviewed bounds are refused before reserving. No numeric supplier prices are invented or embedded in production defaults.

## Financial and operating limits

This does NOT certify the required 60% final net margin after all company and personal owner taxes. It is a provider-wide ceiling based on declared request upper bounds, not a funded per-customer wallet, actual-cost ledger, all-supplier budget or completed tax calculation. Payment fees, email, storage, transfer, compute/database, supplier minimums, support, acquisition, refunds, fixed costs and tax remain separate. In particular, the monetary guard's own Redis requests have costs outside the Anthropic cap.

No alert-dependent safety is used: missing/expired policy stops new model calls. However, automatic issuance/renewal of approved, financially funded policies is not built. Monthly expiry must NOT become a manual monitoring task for the owner; rollout requires a trusted budget issuer from actual collected receipts, verified costs, tax reserves and customer envelopes, with a fixed-cost reserve. Without that issuer the system safely becomes unavailable, rather than satisfying the complete operating requirement. A valid policy is not permission to assume enough cash exists.

Before release: establish conservative provider evidence for each enabled model/content type and output bound; install provider-side limits where effective; reconcile counter continuity; integrate customer-level funded reservations; validate empty-policy and exhaustion UX; review any changed published entitlement/terms; and test actual configured provider eligibility in a nonproduction account. No Stripe fee switch, price, customer subscription or supplier account setting is changed here. Existing pricing remains the owner's specified target configuration.

## Tests

The test runner explicitly supplies a large synthetic budget with one microUSD per mocked request. These are test fixtures, not production cost assumptions or a bypass in application code. A dedicated suite overrides them to verify missing/expired policy, invalid money/basis/evidence/model bounds, malformed and applied-but-lost acknowledgements, shared/concurrent exhaustion, provider failures, payload limits and policy changes/expiry while reserving. Tests make no real supplier calls.
