## Original sources read

- [SII Resolution 107](https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/resoluciones/2024/reso107.pdf), November 14, 2024, especially section 1: addresses nonresident service suppliers under the simplified regime. F129 requires transaction counts, taxable base and reductions. Periods without services, or only non-taxable/exempt services, do not require the form; service supplies only to local VAT taxpayers have a separate information-list qualification. This is not a blanket exemption from every reporting duty.
- [SII Resolution 84](https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/resoluciones/2025/reso84.pdf), July 10, 2025, considerations 4-5 and operative section 1: distinguishes digital intermediation platforms, exclusions for businesses providing only advertising/payment processing, transaction-party VAT status and the payment-authorizing platform where multiple platforms participate. It covers registration for platform operators as well as the specified low-value-goods sellers. A merchant's own registration does not establish the platform's compliance. We do not classify Marktmorgen through this note.
- [SII Resolution 93](https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/resoluciones/2025/reso93.pdf), July 30, 2025, sections 1.3, 5 and final effectiveness clause: effective October 25, 2025 for the specified remote sales and intermediation of third-party services. F129 separates goods/services columns. The period is one or three consecutive months; filing is from the 1st through the 20th of the next month. Currency is elected: US dollars, euros or Chilean pesos. Changes ordinarily use January 21-31 and require no outstanding tax, interest or fine, with a specified exceptional first-change case. There is no form for periods with no taxable operations under this regime. Foreign-currency payments use SWIFT and remain debt until Treasury validation; filing is not confirmed payment. Corrections follow their own procedure, with evidence and late charges where applicable. Actual periods, exceptions, validation and obligations need review, not a calculated LLC calendar.
- [Current SII VAT portal](https://www.sii.cl/vat/index.html), read November 5, 2026: distinguishes the 2020 service regime, the November 2024 expansion and the October 2025 goods changes. It offers separate filing-status/debt tools, registration and information-list paths. An online filing facility is not a supplier quote or evidence of a funded reserve.

The linked Spanish resolutions are the original regulatory sources. SII's English PDFs for [107](https://www.sii.cl/vat/resolucion_107_2024_eng.pdf), [84](https://www.sii.cl/vat/directive_84_2025_eng.pdf) and [93](https://www.sii.cl/vat/directive_93_2025_eng.pdf) expressly identify themselves as non-authoritative free translations; they were read as aids, not substitutes. This note does not assert that every supplier uses the platform procedure or that older service instructions alone resolve the later platform rules.
