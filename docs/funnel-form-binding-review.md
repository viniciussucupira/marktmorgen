# Saved funnel form-binding review

Studio extends the before-sharing checklist to form blocks on existing, currently visible site-page steps, including both saved versions of a page test. A block without a selection and a confirmed missing form produce repair findings linked to the existing page editor. An incomplete, malformed, oversized or failed read stays unknown. This does not validate submissions, branching/scoring, marketing consent, product recommendations, checkout or delivery.

One shared review promise serves all saved funnels. Page definitions are deduplicated into one GET pipeline, followed only when necessary by one HMGET of selected form definitions. The existing ten-page limit bounds page commands; at most twenty distinct form definitions are read per review. Further references remain unknown with an explicit limit message. No answers, contacts, exports or activity records are read. Hidden/scheduled pages retain their existing visibility findings and are checked for bindings after becoming visible.

Page records use existing block and stored-byte limits. Received form definitions over 64 KiB are refused before JSON parsing. This is a parsing safeguard, not a guarantee about bytes already transferred from storage. Query counts and existing capacity limits bound this implementation; they do not establish a funded monthly spend envelope or certify the required after-all-tax net margin. Prices, entitlements and commercial gates are unchanged.

Page/form definitions can change between reads or after the review. The checklist is advisory, not an atomic publication authorization or live provider probe. Refresh and test the complete customer journey before sharing. No form response is submitted, marketing message sent, model invoked or payment initiated.
