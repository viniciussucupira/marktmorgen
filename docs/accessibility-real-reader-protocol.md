# AX2 real screen reader and device test protocol

Prepared 23 October 2026. This is a protocol, not an executed audit or a conformity certificate. Reuse the consent, recruitment, accommodations, neutral prompts, privacy controls and funding formula in docs/accessibility-real-people-plan.md; do not duplicate or weaken that programme.

## Safety and evidence

Use an isolated disposable deployment with local simulated Stripe, email and storage and synthetic study identities. Record the exact application commit, route/state, OS, browser, screen reader/version, device, viewport, zoom, input method and Fortaleza start/end times. No participant/customer data, live payment, real email, production record, account connection or public business is required. Stop on any accidental external write or privacy exposure.

For each step record the exact spoken name, role, state and live announcement, keyboard/touch action, focus destination/order, expected result, actual result, unassisted/assisted/not completed, blocking criterion and consented evidence. Never replace speech with DOM accessibility snapshots. A missing/incorrect announcement fails that step. Record not tested and blocked separately from pass. Preserve transient announcements as well as final output.

## Task script

1. Signup and access: locate the signup/login entry; hear the page heading and email field label; use a study inbox; trigger a deliberate invalid entry and locate the error; explicitly request a study link, open the captured local message, confirm and reach the empty Studio. Confirm that requesting a link alone creates no paid plan or marketing consent.
2. Create a store: find the address/name controls, hear instructions, test an unavailable or invalid synthetic address, correct it, explicitly create the disposable store and verify saved identity after reload.
3. Create and publish a product: name a harmless synthetic product, price and limits; hear field names, required/invalid states and save result; reload, preview, explicitly publish and find the resulting product anonymously. Unpublish afterwards. Preserve all creator content and persisted identifiers.
4. Buy as a buyer: find the disposable product and explain price, optional extras and applicable conditions; complete only simulated checkout; hear pending/error/success states; use captured local receipt/library link and verify the purchased product. No card or real payment.
5. Enter a course: open the synthetic purchased course, hear course/lesson names and progress, navigate lessons and media controls, locate captions/transcript where applicable, and return without losing place. An absent fixture or unsupported media is not a pass.
6. Cancel a subscription: use a synthetic existing membership/plan, find cancellation, hear consequences and effective date, cancel explicitly, hear the result and verify the synthetic stored state after reload. Confirm previously paid access follows the actual terms, not an assumed immediate cutoff.
7. Request a refund: find the synthetic eligible order, hear amount, eligibility, access consequences and confirmation; test cancel/back before submission, explicitly request the simulated refund once, hear pending or confirmed result honestly and verify access/state only after confirmed refund. Repeated activation must not duplicate a refund; no real refund.

Run each task with keyboard-only desktop navigation and with native touch reader gestures on the relevant physical device. Include empty, loading, validation error, provider error, pending, success, disabled controls, menus/modals open and closed, focus restoration and dismissal where the real task supports them. Mark inapplicable states with a concrete reason rather than fabricating a success.
