## 1. Delivery order and evidence

Follow the requested order: research → feature comparison → economic prioritization → one feature per tested PR → matched click measurements → complete worst-case margin review → honest public comparison.

This package covers a public-source research pass, a 70-capability matrix and an actionable implementation order. It does **not** certify exhaustive private product behavior, matched click superiority, comparative speed, complete migration compatibility, legal compliance or the required net margin. Those remain explicit acceptance gates rather than invented findings. A missing capability in this audit is not proof that either platform lacks it.

- **V**: the linked official document describes the stated capability; it is not an independent performance/security certification.
- **V-index**: the official help index lists the feature, but its complete behavior/entitlement was not exercised.
- **C/U**: implementation located in the pinned Marktmorgen source; full customer journey still unverified.
- **D**: the narrowly stated behavior has recorded release/test evidence; it does not establish complete feature parity.
- **U**: not established. **P**: proposed. Scores and targets are estimates, not measured sales uplift or profit forecasts.
- The JSON companion records sources, access dates, feature evidence and code blob SHAs. No proprietary vendor code, text, images or templates are imported.

The deployed offer uses Store/Studio/Scale labels and the specified public prices. Billing remains independently gated: `COMMISSION_LIVE=false` and paid opening is closed in the inspected snapshot. Historical subscription identities/prices are retained. Publishing an offer does not prove its collection routes or profitability.

## 3. Product organization, technology and strategic interpretation

The help taxonomy exposes funnels, page editing, products/orders, customers, site/blog, courses, community, appointments, affiliates, email, automation, MessageHub and workspace administration. That describes the documented product surface, not a complete measured menu tree. [S28](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/)

Building is integrated: reusable starting points connect pages, products, checkout and follow-up. AI already includes Quick and Research modes, page/copy generation and iterative refinement. Therefore, “AI funnel creation” alone is not a unique Marktmorgen invention. [S03](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/build-with-ai/)

The developer surface distinguishes management REST API, browser Page SDK, human-authorized MCP and webhooks. They are different contracts, not interchangeable checkout APIs. In particular, management REST is not a “charge this card” endpoint for external checkout. [S14](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/which-clickfunnels-integration-should-i-use/)

The October 16 changelog adds editor rotation, reserved-field warnings and scheduling refinements; September records AI/MCP availability and external/custom-HTML page support. Treat releases as vendor documentation, not measured performance evidence. [S26](https://changelog.myclickfunnels.com/)

Private backend architecture, database topology, failover, audited availability and proprietary security controls are unknown. Do not invent the competitor's stack. Marktmorgen's inspectable architecture includes Next.js/TypeScript, separate server integration boundaries, Redis-backed records and creator-owned payment connections; source existence alone does not prove every live credential, provider approval or complete operational assurance.

**Strategic inference:** Marktmorgen should win through a coherent creator journey, original reusable setups, trustworthy money/availability data, predictable limits and fewer recovery/configuration mistakes. Enterprise breadth, more settings or a longer feature list do not demonstrate better outcomes. Every superiority statement needs a matched test.

**AI:** product facts are the source of truth. Bound input/output, model/tool calls, time, concurrency, retries and every entry point. ## 8. Worst-case economic constraints

Approved public subscription prices remain **Store $22 + 6.25%, Studio $149 + 2.75%, Scale $349 + 0%**; annual prices are **$220/$1,490/$3,490**. Creator sale proceeds are not platform revenue. Stress tests use zero platform commission and simultaneous maximum use.

Let S be earned subscription revenue for the evaluated period and C_all every applicable non-overlapping cost and tax, including allocated fixed obligations, refunds/disputes, provider use, support, platform affiliate commissions and approved business/owner tax treatment. Required profit is at least 0.60 × S, so C_all must be at most 0.40 × S. Use the stricter contracted customer-payment basis if applicable; never silently remove an expense or indirect-tax obligation to improve the percentage.
