## Primary sources and version limits

- [National Law Information Center, Korean Article 53-2](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLawLinkInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsId=001571&lsJoLnkSeq=1000920308&print=print): the retrieved page identifies Act No. 21065, promulgated October 1, 2025, effective January 2, 2026. Paragraph (1) covers specified foreign electronic services, including software, advertising, cloud and prescribed intermediation, excluding supplies for registered businesses' taxable or exempt activities. Qualifying suppliers register within 20 days of commencing business. Paragraph (2) assigns supply to specified third-party marketplaces/intermediaries, with its own exclusions; payment collection/remittance is one listed category, not the only one. Paragraph (4) applies the returns/payments of Articles 48(1)-(2) and 49. Paragraph (6) requires transaction details, including supplies for registered businesses, for five years after the final-return deadline for the relevant taxable period. Paragraphs (7)-(8) permit an authority request for transaction details and require submission within 60 days after a request. Paragraph (10) delegates further procedure to the decree. The retrieved article alone does not verify every implementing rule, amendment or transaction's treatment.
- [KLRI English reference translation](https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_mobile/viewer.do?hseq=63471&key=20&type=part): this historical version lists amendments through Act No. 19194 of December 31, 2022. It is reference only, not the current Korean controlling text or proof that subsequent changes are absent. Its Article 53-2 supports comparing the above provisions. Its domestic simplified-taxpayer regime in Chapter VII is distinct from foreign simplified business registration; a generic domestic annual filing row cannot establish a foreign supplier's calendar.
- [NTS VAT on Electronic Services](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/na/ntt/selectNttList.do?bbsId=30699&mi=11210): two explanatory video entries dated December 23, 2020 identify Hometax's simplified registration/filing service. Only the text index was read; video contents and a signed-in filing were not verified. These old entries do not establish today's complete procedure or its price.
