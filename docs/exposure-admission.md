# Persistent company exposure admission

AI requests for trial, setup and ended stores now claim their full reviewed company exposure allocation before AI spending and provider fetch. Ended stores use the post-cancellation scope. The allocation covers the complete reviewed per-case loss, not only the particular AI call. Other calls by the same store in the same scope and UTC month reuse the receipt. Different scopes remain separate obligations.

The funding policy must match the exact current commercial terms and approved exposure review, including every cost category, tax ceiling, occurrence cap and evidence. It also requires explicit reserve evidence and enough reserved cents for the full reviewed company exposure ceiling. The final paid-sales opening guard also requires this exact reserve configuration; a hypothetical passing financial calculation cannot open sales without it. The policy and actual commercial review remain null. No bank balance, tax treatment or profitability is certified by this code.

A single persistent Redis hash holds the review binding, company reserved cents, remaining slots per scope and immutable case receipts. Lua validates every relevant value before writing. It refuses missing, expiring, wrong-type, inconsistent, exhausted or mismatched ledgers. Slots and money are consumed together; retries reuse receipts, and lost acknowledgements retain allocations. This path never initializes, expires, refunds or resets funding. Provider failures and unavailable AI spend budgets cannot free the exposure reservation.

The actual Lua executes against a disposable loopback Redis in CI, including 64-store races, repeated requests, lost replies, shared money ceilings, corrupt receipts and finite zero-cost cases. Provider-path tests use synthetic financial evidence and mocked responses; they demonstrate admission order and refusal, not real billing or commercial readiness.

This is the first resource integration. New enrollment, renewals, full refund principal cash, non-payment, pauses, accrued-credit redemption, video, file delivery, upload, mail and public request costs still need appropriate reserve hooks and independently enforced vendor ceilings. Existing contractual rights and legal refunds must be funded and migrated rather than silently forfeited. Reviewed migrations must reconcile the same money across funding pools and months, with actual available reserve evidence. A monthly exposure policy cannot prove funding for an annual contract or its retention/refund tail. Active retained paid-base observation remains unfinished. Paid sales stay closed.
