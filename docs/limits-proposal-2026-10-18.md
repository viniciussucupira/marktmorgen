## How the cuts are chosen

Lines are cut in this order, each down to at most 10% of today's figure (never below one hour of video or half a GiB of storage) before the next one is touched, and the last one only as far as needed: AI drafts / answers a month → Video hours watched a month → Storage → Orders a month → Store emails a month → Access emails a month → List emails a month → Visits a month. First come the lines a store is least likely to reach in full and where competitors give less or nothing (AI on entry plans, video watched, storage); then orders and the emails that go with them; then list emails and visits, which competitors give far more generously or do not limit at all. A proposed figure is rounded down to two significant figures (storage to half a GiB, video to whole hours) and the plan is computed again at the rounded figures. Each line's cost is linear in its figure; only storage's share of what a store keeps after its plan ends is taken off with it, so the proposal never counts on a saving it cannot show.

## AI captions: the price that would keep 60%
