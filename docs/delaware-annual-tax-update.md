# Delaware annual LLC tax — evidence update

Checked 2026-10-16. For the intended LLC's 2026 tax year, plan for **USD 400 per year**, not the older USD 300 figure.

The [Division of Corporations instructions](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/) state USD 400 annually. [HB 400, signed May 21, 2026](https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail?LegislationId=143069), and the [enacted session law, chapter 273](https://www.legis.delaware.gov/SessionLaws/Chapter?id=42436), resolve the conflict with the older consolidated code page: section 23 increases the LLC annual tax, effective January 1, 2026 under section 45. The annual tax is for the prior year and ordinarily due June 1. Confirm the actual formation year and account liability before payment.

Monthly accrual equivalent: USD 400 / 12 = USD 33.333333 for the whole LLC. This is an accrual, not the amount of the cash invoice in its due month. Any shared-product allocation must preserve the whole-company total and avoid double counting. A reserve policy is required if the model smooths annual cash obligations.

This amount excludes the registered agent, accounting, tax filings, infrastructure, owner compensation, startup expenses and all other liabilities. It cannot establish a complete fixed-cost ceiling or a real minimum customer base. Unknown costs remain unknown.
