## Legal distinctions retained for funding

Article 35 provides quarterly reporting as the ordinary rule, half-yearly reporting for the specified net-tax-rate method, and other requested periods under its conditions. Article 86a provides provisional instalment funding with annual reporting. Therefore annual filing does not mean annual-only cash funding. The correct method, periods, due-date adjustments, instalments, reconciliation and payment evidence for this LLC remain unverified.

Article 67(1bis) allows the FTA to waive appointment of a representative when procedural obligations and effective implementation are otherwise secured, subject to special laws. The public guidance still describes appointment; a waiver is not presumed. A possible waiver neither proves zero representation/compliance cost nor authorises a request or appointment. Article 67(3) distinguishes appointment from creation of a permanent establishment for direct taxes; it does not decide the LLC's actual establishment facts.

Article 70 ties relevant records to the absolute assessment limitation in Article 42(6), ten years after the end of the tax period concerned, and reserves the accounting-law rule. Article 70(3) specifies 20 years for the stated immovable-property adjustment records. These are not a universal deletion date or permission to retain unrelated personal data. Article 72 requires identified annual-account errors to be corrected by the return for the period containing the 180th day after the relevant financial year ends; earlier-period errors have their own finality/limitation conditions. This unit creates no storage, deletion schedule or filing software.
