## Owner requirement: customer-level net margin

Every paying customer, taken one at a time and using every entitlement of its plan at the maximum in the worst month (commission on sales counted as zero), must meet a minimum 60% net margin after all applicable taxes and all variable costs. Do not use average customer consumption or cross-subsidies to pass an unprofitable customer. Include annual discounts and overages. Never count a supplier's free quota as zero cost; use the paid unit price at its most expensive tier. If a plan misses 60%, fix it by REDUCING that plan's limits, never by changing its price, and enforce the limits automatically with per-supplier spending ceilings: the owner will not watch dashboards or act on alerts. (For Marktmorgen, the owner's decision of 18 October 2026, 11:21, below, replaces "never by changing its price": prices may change, visible limits may not be cut.)

The owner's own rule (16 Oct 2026) puts two things outside the per-customer unit, and both stay mandatory:

- **Fixed costs** (tool subscriptions, domains, the LLC's compliance costs, split 50/50 with Hazelsong) are computed separately as the minimum number of paying customers needed to cover them. The whole company must reach 60% after all US and Brazilian taxes in the worst case (everyone using everything at once); release needs that minimum base, never a smaller one.
- **Free trial, the money-back refund, non-payment and post-cancellation retention** are funded exposures: each has a calculated maximum cost, is kept low by limits, is bounded by occurrence caps and is paid from a reviewed company exposure ceiling added to the fixed costs above (docs/funded-exposure-review.md). They are never waived and never offset by another scope's profit.

Unknown taxes, unbounded costs and missing reviews do not count as zero: they keep release closed. Use 27.5% (the Brazilian individual top rate) as the minimum profit-tax stress rate, and a higher combined rate wherever the reading with more tax gives one (docs/tax-uncertainty.md); since 18 Oct 2026 the owner uses no accountant, so a lower reading needs an official text or a recorded consultation answer. Paid commercial release stays closed while the review in lib/commercial-margin.ts is absent or fails. Existing operating-margin tests are not proof of all-tax net margin. Do not claim that this requirement is satisfied merely because a guard or a calculator was added.

## Marktmorgen as the supplier for tax: EU art. 9a and the rest (18 Oct 2026)

Council Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 art. 9a presumes that a platform taking part in a supply of electronically supplied services acts in its own name, and the presumption cannot be rebutted by one that authorises the charge, authorises the delivery or sets the general terms and conditions. Marktmorgen does all three, so for EU VAT it is the supplier of what a store sells electronically to an EU consumer, whatever the terms say and although every sale is a direct charge on the creator's account: VAT at the consumer's rate on the whole price, the non-Union OSS from the first sale, two non-contradictory pieces of location evidence (arts. 24b, 24f) and the art. 63c records kept 10 years. The same is taken, under the reading with more tax, for the UK, US states' marketplace-facilitator laws and other countries' platform rules. The owner's design: the tax is added on top of the buyer's price or taken from the creator's payout, never from Marktmorgen's commission. None of it is built ([docs/eu-vat-article-9a.md](docs/eu-vat-article-9a.md) has the reading per checkout path and the next build). Until each jurisdiction has a complete record in `DEEMED_SUPPLIER_RECORDS` (`lib/deemed-supplier.ts`), `PLANS_ON_SALE` (`lib/opening.ts`) is false, and so no store can sell (`canSell` needs a plan); `tests/tax-gate.test.ts` names art. 9a among the reasons. Never record a treatment that is not built and tested.
