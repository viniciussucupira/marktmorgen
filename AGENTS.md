## Owner requirement: customer-level net margin

Every paying customer, taken one at a time and using every entitlement of its plan at the maximum in the worst month (commission on sales counted as zero), must meet a minimum 60% net margin after all applicable taxes and all variable costs. Do not use average customer consumption or cross-subsidies to pass an unprofitable customer. Include annual discounts and overages. Never count a supplier's free quota as zero cost; use the paid unit price at its most expensive tier. If a plan misses 60%, fix it by REDUCING that plan's limits, never by changing its price, and enforce the limits automatically with per-supplier spending ceilings: the owner will not watch dashboards or act on alerts. (For Marktmorgen, the owner's decision of 18 October 2026, 11:21, below, replaces "never by changing its price": prices may change, visible limits may not be cut.)

The owner's own rule (16 Oct 2026) puts two things outside the per-customer unit, and both stay mandatory:

- **Fixed costs** (tool subscriptions, domains, the LLC's compliance costs, split 50/50 with Hazelsong) are computed separately as the minimum number of paying customers needed to cover them. The whole company must reach 60% after all US and Brazilian taxes in the worst case (everyone using everything at once); release needs that minimum base, never a smaller one.
- **Free trial, the money-back refund, non-payment and post-cancellation retention** are funded exposures: each has a calculated maximum cost, is kept low by limits, is bounded by occurrence caps and is paid from a reviewed company exposure ceiling added to the fixed costs above (docs/funded-exposure-review.md). They are never waived and never offset by another scope's profit.

Unknown taxes, unbounded costs and missing reviews do not count as zero: they keep release closed. Use 27.5% (the Brazilian individual top rate) as the minimum profit-tax stress rate, and a higher combined rate wherever the reading with more tax gives one (docs/tax-uncertainty.md); since 18 Oct 2026 the owner uses no accountant, so a lower reading needs an official text or a recorded consultation answer. Paid commercial release stays closed while the review in lib/commercial-margin.ts is absent or fails. Existing operating-margin tests are not proof of all-tax net margin. Do not claim that this requirement is satisfied merely because a guard or a calculator was added.

How it is applied, with the two decisions the owner made the same night when asked where the literal rule cannot be guaranteed by code:

## Tax uncertainty: the reading with more tax (owner, 17 Oct 2026, rule 19)

Where the law is unclear or has more than one reading, every margin model, review and gate uses the reading that gives MORE tax, so profit is never overstated; the questions, their status (CLOSED-TEXT, CLOSED-DESIGN, CLOSABLE-CONSULTA, OPEN), citations and readings are in [docs/tax-uncertainty.md](docs/tax-uncertainty.md). **Since 18 Oct 2026 the owner uses no accountant:** a doubt is closed only by an official text or by an answer to a consultation (Receita under IN RFB 2.058/2021, or the Prefeitura de Fortaleza for ISS), recorded with its date and source ([docs/tax-consultations.md](docs/tax-consultations.md), `CONSULTATION_ANSWERS` in `lib/tax-gate.ts`); otherwise it stays at the most-tax reading. The gate reading is `gateReading()` (`lib/tax-gate.ts`): `MOST_TAX_READING` while `EU_HOSTING_CONFIRMED` is false (it is set only once both products' servers are in EU regions), under which the 60% floor is not reachable at any limit, so paid sales stay closed; `tests/tax-gate.test.ts` fails if the old confirmation flag comes back or anything is on sale while a published plan misses 60% under the gate reading. Never lower the tax assumption or loosen a gate to make a test pass, and never raise a price to pass the most-tax gate; limits and prices are sized for 60% at the most favorable reading the owner may later confirm (the sizing reading).

## Marktmorgen as the supplier for tax: EU art. 9a and the rest (18 Oct 2026)

Council Implementing Regulation (EU) No 282/2011 art. 9a presumes that a platform taking part in a supply of electronically supplied services acts in its own name, and the presumption cannot be rebutted by one that authorises the charge, authorises the delivery or sets the general terms and conditions. Marktmorgen does all three, so for EU VAT it is the supplier of what a store sells electronically to an EU consumer, whatever the terms say and although every sale is a direct charge on the creator's account: VAT at the consumer's rate on the whole price, the non-Union OSS from the first sale, two non-contradictory pieces of location evidence (arts. 24b, 24f) and the art. 63c records kept 10 years. The same is taken, under the reading with more tax, for the UK, US states' marketplace-facilitator laws and other countries' platform rules. The owner's design: the tax is added on top of the buyer's price or taken from the creator's payout, never from Marktmorgen's commission. None of it is built ([docs/eu-vat-article-9a.md](docs/eu-vat-article-9a.md) has the reading per checkout path and the next build). Until each jurisdiction has a complete record in `DEEMED_SUPPLIER_RECORDS` (`lib/deemed-supplier.ts`), `PLANS_ON_SALE` (`lib/opening.ts`) is false, and so no store can sell (`canSell` needs a plan); `tests/tax-gate.test.ts` names art. 9a among the reasons. Never record a treatment that is not built and tested.

## One receiving model for every unit (18 Oct 2026)

`lib/receiving-costs.ts` is the only model of what receiving a payment costs, for every plan, interval and add-on, aligned with Hazelsong's `WORST_CASE`: the dearer per-charge fee of Stripe with every surcharge (6.6% + 30¢) and Paddle (5% + 50¢) ON THE AMOUNT CHARGED (the price plus the buyer's tax on top at 27%; a line on a renewal invoice pays only the percentages), remittance 1.5% of revenue, a 1% refund reserve, a dispute reserve of ($30 + amount charged) × 1%, and a profit tax never below 27.5%. `tests/plan-cost-model.ts` (`netOf`), `tests/spend-floor.test.ts` and the add-on read it; no other fee figure may be typed. Under it no unit keeps 60% at full use today, and none can by cutting a limit the customer never sees (the "Printed lines only" column). The owner's decision of 18 Oct 2026 (morning): no cut to any limit a customer sees (visits, orders, video hours, storage, AI drafts, emails, anything printed) and no change to the number of plans; its price rule was replaced at 11:21 (next section). `docs/margin-alignment-2026-10-18.md` is generated by `node scripts/margin-alignment.mjs` and `tests/margin-alignment.test.ts` fails when it is stale, when a unit on sale misses 60%, when anything is on sale while a unit misses, or when the most-tax reading passes any unit. Never lower a tax or fee assumption or loosen a gate to make it pass.

- **Margin.** The add-on alone, every reply at its worst, must keep 60% after the 27.5% stress tax with the dearer provider's fee on the amount charged (6.6% + 30¢ on the price plus the buyer's tax on top at 27%), remittance, dispute and refund reserves, and at least `ADD_ON_ROOM_CENTS` of room (`ADD_ON_LINES` in tests/plan-cost-registry.ts, tests/store-assistant.test.ts), all from `lib/receiving-costs.ts`. At $49 it kept 59.06%; at $69 (owner, 18 October 2026, 11:21) it keeps 60.40% with its room (`assistantMeetsFloor()` true) and is still not on sale, because `ASSISTANT_ON_SALE` needs `PLANS_ON_SALE` (docs/margin-alignment-2026-10-18.md). Under the most-tax reading it fails and stays closed. Its terms are in `CURRENT_COMMERCIAL_TERMS.storeAssistant`.
- **Cost bound.** One model only, `ASSISTANT_MODEL` (the cheapest current, Haiku 5.5), not a deployment setting; input capped at `ASSISTANT_INPUT_BYTES`, output at `ASSISTANT_OUTPUT_TOKENS`, so a message's worst cost is `assistantMessageWorstMicroUsd()`. The pool (`ASSISTANT_POOL`) is reserved before every model call, settled at the billed tokens, released only when the provider answered with an error status, kept whole on a timeout. A spent pool turns the chat into the no-AI "leave your question" form. Never call the model for the assistant without that reservation.
- **Honesty.** Every reply is shown under `ASSISTANT_LABEL`, drawn by the page and sent by the server, never by the model. It answers only from the store's published facts (`assistantFacts`); a reply with a number, a deal or pressure word, a code or a link not in those facts is never shown (`heldToFacts`). It never writes as the creator. No invented testimonials, figures, scarcity or urgency; no discount or term the creator did not publish.
- **Hand-over.** Complaints, refunds, payment problems and requests for a person go to the creator without a model call (`HANDOFF_WORDS`) or on the model's `handoff`; the question, the conversation and the buyer's typed email go into the existing inbox (`lib/inbox.ts`, `handOver`), from the plan's own inbox and store-email figures.
- **Prompt injection.** The rules live only in the system prompt; the buyer's words are quoted data with `<`/`>` neutralized and addresses, phones and links removed. The tests that a model obeying an injection is never shown must keep passing.
- **Privacy.** The conversation is kept `ASSISTANT_KEEP_SECONDS` (24 h) under a random token, with addresses, phones and links removed, deletable by the buyer at once; unanswered questions never say who asked. `lib/person-data.ts`, the privacy policy, the terms and the refund policy say so; change them together.
- **Billing shape.** Its own monthly subscription with `metadata[addon]=assistant` and `metadata[assistant_for]`, never `metadata[store]`, so nothing that reads plans can take it for one.
- **Next product after a purchase (function 2: `lib/next-offer*.ts`, `components/next-offer-*.tsx`, `app/api/store/assistant-next`).** One product, only the creator's own that the store lists and sells today, at its published price and terms; never the one bought, anything in the order, anything turned down after paying, a draft, a limited one, a bundle holding something owned, or anything the address bought here before (read from the creator's Stripe account and imported purchases; unknown means nothing is shown). The creator's own choices decide first (picks, offers after paying, checkout boxes, collections); the model is asked only when there are none and at least two candidates, from the same `ASSISTANT_POOL` at the same worst-cost reservation as a chat message, settled at billed tokens, released when nothing was billed; a spent pool falls back to the rule with no model. The "AI suggestion" label is drawn by the page, never by the model; the model's sentence is shown only when `heldToFacts` passes. No discount, countdown, scarcity, count of buyers or testimonial. Never shown while an offer after paying (`lib/upsell.ts`) waits on an answer; the buyer can dismiss it; one choice per order, kept `NEXT_OFFER_KEEP_SECONDS`. Its reads are `NEXT_OFFER_REQUEST_UNITS` public requests (held over `NEXT_OFFER_READ` in tests/plan-cost-registry.test.ts).
